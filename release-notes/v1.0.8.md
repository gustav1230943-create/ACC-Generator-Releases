## ACC Generator v1.0.8

**No license key needed anymore** — the key system is switched off. The app starts directly again, like before v1.0.7.

Everything new from v1.0.7 is included:

- **Multitool layout** with sidebar (*Settings → Design & Fenster → Oberfläche*): label & tags, Random / Passphrase, bulk generate, copy all as CSV, history with search, sorting, tag filter, ★ favorites and edit, mini mode
- **7 color designs**: Original, Fluent, ImGui, NiceGUI, Material, Terminal, Multitool
- **Window transparency** 0–100 % (Ctrl+0 resets to 0 %)

**Stuck at the license window in v1.0.7?** Download `Acc.Generator.exe` below and replace your old exe. Your saved accounts, notes and settings stay.
